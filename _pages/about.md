I am a third-year Computer Science student at the Informatics Institute of Technology, affiliated with the University of Westminster. I am passionate about high-performance machine learning and numerical computing.

Currently, I am a Trainee Software Engineer (R&D), specializing in the integration of advanced AI technologies into robust software architectures. My work focuses on building sophisticated conversational agents and architecting high-performance Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG) pipelines. I leverage modern standards like the Model Context Protocol (MCP) to build scalable tool-use capabilities and utilize orchestration frameworks such as LangChain. By mastering advanced prompt engineering and vector database optimization, I develop end-to-end solutions for complex, real-world challenges in LLM-powered applications.

My technical interests center on the "under-the-hood" aspects of machine learning. I am deeply interested in high-performance model development, specifically leveraging the JAX ecosystem and frameworks like Flax NNX. I enjoy the challenge of bridging the gap between mathematical theory and hardware-optimized code, exploring how low-level implementations in C++ can maximize the efficiency of machine learning libraries.

Beyond AI, I have a strong foundation in building scalable, performance-critical applications. I am particularly proficient in Go, leveraging its high-concurrency capabilities, alongside experience in Java (Spring Boot). While I am versatile across modern frontend and mobile stacks, my primary focus is architecting seamless integrations between user interfaces and highly optimized backend services. I am also experienced in DevOps, utilizing Docker, Kubernetes, and CI/CD to deploy and scale robust systems. I also value collaborative development, having contributed to open-source software projects to refine my engineering skills and support community tools.

Driven by a goal to become an AI Researcher, I am deeply committed to the academic evolution of the field. I actively track state-of-the-art preprints and emerging technologies to stay at the forefront of this fast-moving industry. My focus lies in exploring novel machine learning architectures and optimization techniques, with the ultimate goal of developing high-efficiency solutions that solve complex real-world problems and shape the future of computational intelligence.

Pushing the boundaries of conventional computing, I am expanding my expertise into Quantum Computing and specialized AI hardware. I was a participant in the Quantum Quest 2025 initiative and am currently navigating IBM’s Quantum ecosystem to grasp the potential of quantum algorithms. Simultaneously, I investigate the hardware that powers modern AI, studying the architecture of Google TPUs, advanced GPU structures, and the engineering behind the hyperscale data centers that drive the industry's exponential growth.
